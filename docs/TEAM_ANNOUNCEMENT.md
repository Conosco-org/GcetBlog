# 🎉 New Features Available - UI Improvements Update

**Date**: April 17, 2026  
**Audience**: Editors & Contributors

---

## 📢 What's New?

We've made several improvements to make your content creation and management experience better!

---

## 🎯 For Editors

### 1. **Preview Your Posts Before Publishing** ✨
You can now preview how your post will look before publishing!

**How to use**:
- When creating or editing a post, click the **"Preview"** button in the header
- Your draft is automatically saved and opens in a new tab
- Perfect for checking formatting and layout

### 2. **Better Comment Management** 🔍
We've improved how you search and filter comments:

**New Features**:
- **Enhanced Search**: Search by post title, author name, OR comment content (all at once!)
- **Category Filter**: Filter comments by post category
- **Simplified Review Queue**: Card-based layout with simple Approve/Reject buttons

**How to use**:
- Go to Content Manager → Comments tab
- Use the search box and category dropdown to find comments quickly
- In Review Queue, comments now appear as cards (like the drafts page)

### 3. **Filter Media by Uploader** 👤
Find media files faster!

**How to use**:
- Go to Media Manager
- Use the new "User" dropdown to filter by who uploaded the file
- Great for finding your own uploads

### 4. **Quick Access to Activity Logs** 📊
Activity logs are now in the sidebar!

**How to use**:
- Look at the bottom of the sidebar
- Click "Activity Logs" for quick access

### 5. **View Posts Directly from Content Manager** 🔗
Click "View Post" on any post to see it live or preview drafts.

**How to use**:
- Go to Content Manager → Posts tab
- Click the three-dot menu (⋮) on any post
- Click "View Post"

## 🎯 For Contributors

### **Preview Your Drafts Before Submitting** ✨
You can now preview your posts before submitting them for review!

**How to use**:
- When creating or editing a draft, click the **"Preview"** button
- Your draft is saved and opens in a new tab
- Check how it looks, then submit for review when ready

---

## 🎓 Quick Tips

### For Editors:
1. Use the **Preview button** to check post formatting before publishing
2. Use the **Category filter** in comments to manage specific content areas
3. Use the **User filter** in Media Manager to find specific uploads
4. The **Review Queue** is now simpler - just Approve or Reject!

### For Contributors:
1. Always **Preview** your post before submitting for review
2. Make sure everything looks good in the preview
3. Then click "Submit for Review" when ready
